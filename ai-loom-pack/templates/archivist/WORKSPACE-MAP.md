# Workspace Map

- `protocols/`: reviewed retrieval and candidate-review procedures.
- `skills/`: compatibility notes for the installed provenance skill; not skill
  source or an installation destination.
- `investigations/`: bounded evidence, open questions, and review reasoning.
- `handoffs/`: exact IDs, postures, gaps, requested decisions, and validation.

Project source, Notes, Objects, LOOM project registry state, worker state, and
Provenance remain outside this workspace and authoritative in their own
domains. The workspace deliberately has no memory, credential, database,
runtime-state, or task-registry directory.
