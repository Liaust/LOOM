# Company Box Policy Example

This generic Company policy names no organization and grants no account access.
The operator must adopt it for an actual Box and configure its approved human
service account, literal profile, endpoint and verified actor/account binding.
Require that binding before reads or writes and repeat preflight after auth
changes. Missing or ambiguous binding holds that integration, not local coding.

Every Company Basecamp write needs the operator's explicit approval of its
destination, content and state changes, including routine updates, completion,
uploads, schedule changes and remote drafts. Existing tasks, login and generic
coding permission are not exceptions. Unattended work prepares and reports,
never silently publishes. Keep proposals local until approved.

Use the Box-selected full native CLI, not a reduced approval API. On a configured
host use `loom-box-tool --box <Box ID> <tool> ...` for explicitly authorized
cross-Box work. Verify exact actor and current account from structured output.
Never fall back to named-agent credentials, Personal defaults, cookies or
another account. Never alter defaults or authenticate to repair a task refusal.
No Personal task destination or personal-account exception is implied.

An ordinary coding worker remains Codex. Tyler remains Tyler only when his
trusted named bootstrap explicitly establishes him. Account selection does not
change conversational identity or transfer private memory. `TYLER-BASECAMP.md`
and `TYLER-MAIL.md` are optional unconfigured examples, not active exceptions.
Without a separately approved binding and scope, no Personal exception is implied.

Work within the selected Box. Another Box is available only when the operator
explicitly asks; reachability does not authorize a durable copy or publication.
Keep Company context in its owning Box/project, not Personal Notes, Provenance,
memory, learned skills or Basecamp. Retained chats may contain sensitive material:
do not assume cross-Box retention consent or that compaction erases it.
This is soft workflow separation, not an OS sandbox.

Register projects normally in the surrounding workspace when authorized.
Ordinary member repositories need no LOOM SDK or runtime merely for coding.
Use source reads for known files, Notes for discovery and qualified Provenance
when relevant; none transfers authority. Use the current harness's native
tools. ORCA is retired, not a fallback. Preserve learned state, pending edits,
conversations and recovery history. No template activates jobs or services.

Verify approved effects and read back uncertain writes before retrying.
Account access never authorizes publication, email, spending, deployment,
credential changes, deletion or commitments. Report unavailable integrations
without broadening scope, and continue independent authorized work.
