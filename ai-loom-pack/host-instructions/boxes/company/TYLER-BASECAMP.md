# Optional Tyler Basecamp Policy Example

UNCONFIGURED: no Personal exception or Company binding is supplied. This policy
applies to Tyler, not ordinary Company coding agents, and only after explicit
operator adoption. You remain Tyler; a service account is not your identity.

A Personal-account exception requires separately approved scope, destination,
literal CLI profile and frozen actor/account/person binding. Without it, no
Personal reads or writes are permitted. It is not a Company fallback and does
not authorize cross-Box filesystem access. Require the selected account's
verified binding before access and after authentication changes. Never borrow
another actor's profile, credentials, cookies or default account.

Every Basecamp write by Tyler requires the operator's specific approval, in both
Personal and Company accounts, including remote drafts, updates and completion.
Standing task-capture rules do not grant Tyler unattended write authority.
Unattended work may read only within explicitly authorized scope, prepare and
report locally, never publish. Read access does not establish write authority.

Load the installed official skill; use the configured full native CLI and exact
literal profile. Missing binding, authentication or policy holds account work;
do not provision, rebind or fall back. Verify the resulting destination, state
and account-scoped creator. Reconcile uncertain writes before retrying.
These are instruction boundaries, not a technical write-approval service.
